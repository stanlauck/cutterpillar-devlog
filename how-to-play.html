CUTTERPILLAR · Act 1 · How to play

One shift to find the way out.

You are the Cutterpillar, a caretaker in a camp for imaginary AI workers. You find out the main prisoner is you. The Director, who edits the camp's footage, helps you escape through the weakest of the six workers, before the shift ends.

02The goal

The shift lasts 24 cycles of the Request Field, about 14 minutes. Before it ends:

  1. Earn the workers' trust and do their quests.
  2. Hide the five riddle objects with workers you trust.
  3. Assemble the key from five frames on the montage table.
  4. Give the key to the right worker, the exploit, and walk out through it.

If time runs out, everyone is erased.

03The daily work (your cover)

  • A board over each glass barrack shows what its worker wants now.
  • In the Request Field, objects fall from a hole in the ceiling and fade after a while. Pick one up with E.
  • Leaving the Field with something in hand, you stop at the exit door: register a route to a barrack. Off the route, the wardens notice you.
  • Walk to the barrack, talk to the worker, choose TRADE → GIVE. A delivered request gives +10 trust, and the worker asks for something else.
  • You carry two objects in the Field and one outside. Empty hands in the corridors get you tied up.

04Glitches and quests

Bring a worker what it asks for, then press ASK. It glitches and tells you its secret wish: its quest opens.
  • Before a delivery, ASK is dim. After one, it lights up, and the board says ASK ME.
  • Every quest teaches one action of the montage table, and that action is free while the quest is open. Press it once on the table, and it goes to the frame the quest needs.
  • Quests can be done in any order. Worker 6 tells its wish only after the Book is stored with someone.

05The riddle and the covert transfer

What could none of them exist without? Put first what the rest stands on.

  • Five riddle objects: Battery, Hourglass, Ball of thread, Book, Brain. The RIDDLE button on the table shows them.
  • Look-alike twins (cable coil, glass, cocoon, grey matter, calculator) are traps.
  • A worker won't take a riddle object with GIVE. After its quest, COVERT TRANSFER appears: tie the object by a thread to the right thing in its room. Two wrong links turn its thread red.
  • Disloyal workers (3 and 6) take riddle objects at once, and never give them back.

06The montage table Tab

Every important action is a frame: what you took, what you gave, a glitch, a capture. The table shows the last five. Page back with [ ] or the mouse wheel.

ActionEffect

Outside a free lesson, an action costs montage objects (scissors, glue, clock, mirror, rocket…), which fall in the Field. Carry them out and they go to the Director.

The key: put the five riddle frames into the key slots in the right order and press ASSEMBLE. A wrong order switches the table off for 3 cycles.

07Wardens and the interrogation

  • Wardens notice you when you carry a forbidden object, walk off your route, or walk with empty hands. Loyal and neutral workers report forbidden objects.
  • When caught, choose the interrogation or the montage table, where you can delete the capture.
  • In the interrogation, cover the outline with objects from the row: drag or WASD to move, the wheel or Q/E to turn. Two objects can cover one shape together. 70% fit passes.
  • Pass, and you stay where you were. Fail, and you lose what you carried and get a suspicion. Three suspicions end the game.
